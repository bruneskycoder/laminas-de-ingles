El pasado (1): simple y continuo

Los hechos de una historia y lo que estaba pasando alrededor.

Mapa: ING-T-01
Estructura: ING-T-02
Sigue en ING-T-07
Past simpleU5
«trabajé», «fui»
+I worked · I went (irregular) −I didn't work · I didn't go ?Did you work? · Did you go?

Palabras señal: yesterday, last week, last year, two years ago, in 2019, when I was…

  1. 1Un hecho terminado, en un momento terminadoI went to Bariloche last year.Fui a Bariloche el año pasado.
  2. 2Una serie de hechos, en ordenI got up, had breakfast and went to work.Me levanté, desayuné y me fui a trabajar.
  3. 3Estados del pasadoWhen I was a child, I lived in a small town.En mi infancia vivía en un pueblo.
Did you went? → Did you go? Con did el verbo vuelve a la forma base: el pasado ya lo lleva did.
Past continuousU6
«estaba trabajando»
+I was working · they were working −I wasn't working · they weren't working ?Were you working? · Was she working?

Palabras señal: while, when, at that moment, at 8 o'clock yesterday, all morning

  1. 1En curso en un momento del pasadoAt 8 o'clock I was having dinner.A las 8 estaba cenando.
  2. 2El fondo de una historia, cortado por un hechoI was working when the phone rang.Estaba trabajando cuando sonó el teléfono.
  3. 3Dos acciones al mismo tiempoWhile I was cooking, my brother was cleaning.Mientras yo cocinaba, mi hermano limpiaba.
«Estaba» no siempre es continuo: «estaba nublado» es It was cloudy (no It was being cloudy); «lo conocía» es I knew him (no I was knowing him).
